## NOTES & SUGGESTIONS

- **Currency:** kept in MXN since the audience is Mexican. If you're targeting U.S. buyers, add an approximate USD figure (≈ $110 USD) so the price reads instantly.
- **Consistency:** the source used both "StarupX" and "StartupX." Standardized to StartupX — confirm the correct spelling.
- **Trust boosters worth adding:** a satisfaction/refund guarantee and a specific count ("Join 200+ agents") would strengthen the offer if the numbers are real. I left these out because I can't verify them — don't invent figures.
